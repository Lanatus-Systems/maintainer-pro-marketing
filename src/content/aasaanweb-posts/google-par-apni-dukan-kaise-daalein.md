# Google par apni dukan kaise daalein – aur website ka link kyun zaroori hai

> **Quick answer:** Google par apni dukan daalne ke liye business.google.com/add par jaiye, "Add your business to Google" chuniye, naam, category, address, phone aur timing bhariye, aur verification poora kijiye. Agar aapki dukan Maps par pehle se dikh rahi hai, to Google Maps mein use kholkar "Claim this business" kijiye. Profile ban jaane ke baad uske **Website** field mein apne business ka page jodiye, taaki customer ko Google se seedha poori jaankari mile.

Aaj customer dukan aane se pehle phone par Google karta hai: "saree shop near me", "namkeen wholesale Indore", "steel bartan Jagadhri". Jo dukan wahan dikhti hai, wahi call aur raasta paati hai. Is guide mein hum dekhenge ki Google par dukan kaise daalte hain, kya bharna zaroori hai, aur website ka link kyun us profile ko poora karta hai.

## Step 1: Pehle check kariye, dukan Google par pehle se hai ya nahi

Google Maps kholiye aur apni dukan ka naam aur shehar search kariye.

- **Dukan nahi dikh rahi:** naya profile banana hoga (Step 2).
- **Dukan dikh rahi hai par aapke control mein nahi:** Google Help ke mutabik, Maps par business kholkar **Claim this business → Manage now** chuniye, phir verification option chuniye. Mobile par Google Maps app mein business kholkar **About → Claim this business** milta hai.
- **Kisi aur ne verify kar rakha hai:** Google par "request ownership" ka tareeka hai; uske liye Google Business Profile Help dekhiye.

## Step 2: Naya Business Profile banaiye

Google Business Profile Help ke steps (31 Oct 2026 ko check kiye):

1. **business.google.com/add** par jaiye.
2. **Add your business to Google** chuniye.
3. Screen par jo poocha jaaye, apne business ki details bhariye.
4. Verification option chuniye aur steps poore kijiye.

Dhyan rahe: Google sirf un businesses ko profile deta hai jo uski eligibility guidelines poori karte hain, aur address sahi hona chahiye. Ghar se kaam karte hain aur customer ko address nahi dikhana, to Google address chhupane ka option bhi deta hai.

## Step 3: Profile mein kya-kya bharein

Google Help ke hisaab se in cheezon ko sahi aur taaza rakhiye:

| Field | Kya likhein | Chhoti tip |
|---|---|---|
| Business name | Board par jo naam hai, wahi | Naam mein keywords mat bhariye |
| Category | Aapka main kaam (jaise "Saree store", "Namkeen manufacturer") | Pehli category hi primary hoti hai; 9 aur jod sakte hain |
| Address / service area | Poora aur sahi address; ghar-ghar service dete hain to service area | Dukan shift ki to dobara verify karna padta hai |
| Hours | Roz ki timing, aur tyohaar ke liye special hours | Diwali, Chhath ya Sunday ki alag timing zaroor daaliye |
| Phone | Main number, chahein to 2 aur | Mobile ya landline dono chalte hain |
| Photos | Dukan ka bahar ka photo, andar ka, products | Bahar ka photo customer ko dukan pehchaanne mein madad karta hai |
| Description | Aap kya bechte/banate hain, kab se kaam kar rahe hain | 750 characters tak; isme link ya offer/rate mat daaliye |
| Website | Aapke business page ka poora link (https:// ke saath) | Link khul raha hai, ye zaroor check kariye |

## Step 4: Website field khaali mat chhodiye

Business Profile mein jagah kam hoti hai. Description mein link ya offer nahi daal sakte, aur kai field har business ke liye nahi milte. Website field wahi jagah hai jahan customer "aur jaankari" ke liye click karta hai. Wahan aapka apna page ho to customer ko ek jagah mil jaata hai:

- aap kya bechte ya banate hain, photos ke saath,
- timing aur address,
- call ya WhatsApp ka button,
- aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal.

Google Help yeh bhi kehta hai ki link mein `http://` ya `https://` ke saath poora URL daaliye, aur aapki site par Googlebot block nahi hona chahiye.

**Ek aam galti:** bahut si dukanon ke profile mein website link hai hi nahi, ya purana link hai jo ab khulta nahi. Customer click karta hai, page nahi khulta, aur woh agli dukan par chala jaata hai. Apne profile ka link mahine mein ek baar khud click karke dekhiye.

## FAQ

### Google par dukan daalne ke liye website hona zaroori hai kya?

Nahi. Business Profile bina website ke bhi ban jaata hai. Par website field khaali rehta hai, aur customer ko photos, poori list aur sawaalon ke jawab ke liye koi apna page nahi milta. Isliye profile ke saath ek simple page jodna behtar hai.

### Google Maps par dukan ki location kaise daalein?

Profile banate waqt poora aur sahi address bhariye; Google usi se Maps par pin dikhata hai. Agar aap customer ko apne address par nahi milte, to address chhupa kar service area set kar sakte hain.

### Dukan pehle se Google par dikh rahi hai, par maine nahi banayi. Kya karun?

Google Maps mein dukan kholiye aur "Claim this business → Manage now" chuniye, phir verification poora kijiye. Agar kisi aur ne pehle verify kar rakha hai, to Google ke "request ownership" tareeke se maangiye.

### Timing ya phone badal gaya to kya karun?

Google Search par apne profile mein "Edit profile" ya Google Maps app mein Business → Edit profile se badaliye. Google kuch badlaav live karne se pehle review karta hai. AasaanWeb page par wahi badlaav normal shabdon mein likhkar preview dekhiye aur publish kariye; chhota badlaav lagbhag 10 minute mein live hota hai.

### Ek page kaafi hai ya badi website chahiye?

Zyada tar chhoti dukanon ke liye ek saaf landing page kaafi hota hai: kaam, photos, timing aur contact. AasaanWeb isi landing page ke liye bana hai, badi multi-page sites ke liye nahi.
