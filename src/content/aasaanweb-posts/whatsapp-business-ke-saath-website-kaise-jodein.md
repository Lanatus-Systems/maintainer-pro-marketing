# WhatsApp Business ke saath website kaise jodein (button + profile link)

> **Quick answer:** Website aur WhatsApp Business ko do taraf se jodiye. (1) Apne number se click-to-chat link banaiye: `https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX` (country code 91 ke saath, bina +, 0, space ya dash). (2) Yeh link apni website par ek saaf "WhatsApp karein" button mein lagaiye, sabse upar aur contact section mein. (3) WhatsApp Business app ke business profile mein **Website** field mein apne page ka link daaliye. Ab Google se aaya customer ek tap mein chat kar sakta hai, aur WhatsApp par aaya customer ek tap mein aapka poora kaam dekh sakta hai.

Chhote business ke zyada tar customer aaj pehle WhatsApp par hi poochte hain: "rate kya hai?", "photo bhejo", "dukan kab tak khuli hai?". Par har baar wahi jawab type karna, wahi 20 photos bhejna, thaka deta hai. Aur jo customer Google ya Instagram se aata hai, use aapka WhatsApp number dhoondhna padta hai. Website aur WhatsApp ko jod dene se dono kaam aasaan ho jaate hain.

## Step 1: Apna WhatsApp click-to-chat link banaiye

WhatsApp Help Center ke "click to chat" feature se koi bhi customer aapka number save kiye bina chat shuru kar sakta hai. Link ka format:

- `https://wa.me/<number>` – number poora international format mein. India ke liye pehle **91**, phir 10 ankon ka mobile number.
- **Sahi:** `https://wa.me/919812345678`
- **Galat:** `https://wa.me/+91-98123 45678` ya `https://wa.me/09812345678` (WhatsApp ke hisaab se +, zero, bracket ya dash mat lagaiye)

Chahein to message pehle se likha hua bhi aa sakta hai. Format: `https://wa.me/919812345678?text=...` jahan `text` ke baad ka message URL-encoded hota hai (space ki jagah `%20`). Jaise:

`https://wa.me/919812345678?text=Namaste%2C%20mujhe%20aapke%20products%20ki%20jaankari%20chahiye`

Customer jab link dabata hai, to chat khulti hai aur yeh line pehle se type hui milti hai. Use bas "send" dabana hai. WhatsApp ke mutabik yeh link phone aur WhatsApp Web, dono par chalta hai.

*(Upar wala number sirf example hai. Apna number daaliye.)*

## Step 2: Website par WhatsApp button lagaiye

Link ban gaya. Ab use website par aisi jagah rakhiye jahan customer ki nazar pehle jaati hai:

| Jagah | Kyun | Button par kya likhein |
|---|---|---|
| Page ke sabse upar (pehli screen) | Phone par customer neeche scroll kiye bina hi chat kar sake | "WhatsApp par poochiye" |
| Products/services ke neeche | Dekhne ke baad hi sawaal aata hai | "Is product ke baare mein WhatsApp karein" |
| Contact section | Address aur timing ke saath | "WhatsApp: 98123 45678" |

Teen chhoti baatein:

- **Number wahi ho jo WhatsApp Business par chal raha hai.** Purana number button par reh gaya to customer kabhi nahi pahunchega.
- **Call button bhi rakhiye.** Kai log, khaaskar bade order wale buyer, seedha call karna pasand karte hain.
- **Button dabakar khud check kariye,** phone par bhi aur computer par bhi.

## Step 3: WhatsApp Business profile mein website ka link daaliye

Ab doosri taraf ka link. WhatsApp Business ke apne resource page ke mutabik business profile mein aap **hours, location, website, contact information** aur catalog jaise details daal sakte hain, taaki customer ko pata rahe ki woh sahi business se baat kar raha hai.

WhatsApp Business app mein apna **Business profile** kholiye (Settings ya Tools ke andar milta hai; app version ke hisaab se naam thoda alag ho sakta hai), **Website** wale hisse mein apne page ka poora link `https://` ke saath daaliye, aur save kariye. Saath hi timing, address aur ek line ka description bhi bhar dijiye.

Fayda: jo customer sirf WhatsApp par aapse juda hai, woh profile kholte hi ek tap mein aapka poora kaam, photos aur timing dekh sakta hai. Har baar 20 photos bhejne ki zaroorat kam ho jaati hai. Chat mein bas page ka link bhejiye.

## Step 4: Ek hi link har jagah

Jab website aur WhatsApp jud gaye, to wahi page link har jagah daaliye:

- **Google Business Profile** ka Website field (kaise, yeh hamari guide mein: [Google par apni dukan kaise daalein](https://aasaanweb.butterneck.com/blog/google-par-apni-dukan-kaise-daalein))
- Instagram/Facebook bio
- Visiting card, bill book aur dukan ke board par QR code
- WhatsApp status aur broadcast message

Customer kahin se bhi aaye, use ek hi jagah poori jaankari aur ek hi WhatsApp button milta hai.

## Step 5: Number, timing ya offer badle to page turant theek kariye

WhatsApp jodne ka sabse bada nuksaan tab hota hai jab page par purani jaankari reh jaati hai: purana number, purani timing, khatam ho chuka offer. Isliye aisa tareeka chuniye jismein yeh chhote badlaav aap khud kar sakein.

AasaanWeb isi ke liye bana hai. Aap apna business, ya koi badlaav, normal shabdon mein likhte hain, jaise:

- "Footer mein phone number purana hai. Naya wala lagao."
- "Phone par booking button sabse upar dikhao."
- "Hum Monday ko band rehte hain. Timing se hata do."

Har badlaav pehle preview par dikhta hai, bilkul waise jaise customer dekhega. Theek lage to aap publish karte hain. Chhote badlaav lagbhag 10 minute mein live ho jaate hain. Aap headline, photo, timing, phone, price, button, FAQs aur proof sections khud badal sakte hain, aur website kaise banti hai yeh jaanna zaroori nahi.

## FAQ

### WhatsApp Business ke saath website kaise banaye?

Pehle ek simple landing page banaiye jismein aapka kaam, photos, timing aur contact ho. Phir us page par apne number ka `wa.me` link ek WhatsApp button mein lagaiye, aur WhatsApp Business profile ke Website field mein us page ka link daaliye. AasaanWeb par aap apna business normal shabdon mein batakar aisa page bana sakte hain.

### wa.me link mein number kaise likhein?

WhatsApp ke hisaab se number poore international format mein, bina +, zero, bracket ya dash ke. India ke liye: `https://wa.me/91` aur uske baad 10 ankon ka mobile number.

### Kya link mein pehle se message likh sakte hain?

Haan. `https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=` ke baad apna message URL-encoded likhiye (space = `%20`). Chat khulne par woh line customer ke text box mein pehle se aa jaati hai.

### Website hai hi nahi, sirf WhatsApp Business hai. Kya woh kaafi nahi?

WhatsApp un logon ke liye badhiya hai jo aapko pehle se jaante hain. Naya customer aksar Google par dhoondhta hai, aur wahan ek apna page hona chahiye jo bataye aap kya karte hain, kahan hain aur kaise sampark karein. Page aur WhatsApp ek doosre ko poora karte hain.

### Number badal gaya to kya karna hoga?

Teen jagah badaliye: website ka WhatsApp button (wa.me link), WhatsApp Business profile aur Google Business Profile. AasaanWeb page par bas likhiye "WhatsApp number badal gaya hai, naya yeh hai", preview check kariye aur publish kariye.
