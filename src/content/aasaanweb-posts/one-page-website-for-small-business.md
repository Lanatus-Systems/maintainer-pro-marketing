# One page website for small business: why it is often enough

**Quick answer:** A one page website is a single page that tells customers what your business does, shows your work, and lets them call or message you. For most small Indian shops, studios, clinics and manufacturers, that one page is enough: customers mostly want to check you are real, see what you offer, and reach you. AasaanWeb is built for exactly this kind of landing page. You describe your business, check the page on a preview, and publish it yourself; small changes go live in about 10 minutes.

## What customers actually look for
When someone finds your business on Google, a directory or a WhatsApp forward, they usually want four things fast: what you sell or make, proof that you are real, your hours and location, and a way to contact you. A single, clear page answers all four without making them click around.

## What to put on your one page
1. **A one-line headline** saying what you do and where. For example: "Custom brass parts in Jamnagar."
2. **A real photo** of your shop, product or work, not a stock image.
3. **Your services or products**, in short lines.
4. **Hours and contact details**, with a button to call.
5. **A few common questions** answered, so customers don't have to ask.
6. **Proof**, such as years in the trade or the kind of work you have done.

## When one page is enough, and when it isn't
One page is usually enough if you run a single shop, clinic, studio or small unit and customers contact you directly to buy or order. You may need more than one page if you sell many products online with a cart and payments, or publish a lot of articles. AasaanWeb focuses on the landing page, so it suits the first case.
