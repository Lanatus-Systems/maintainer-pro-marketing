# Dukan ki website kaise banaye – bina coding, apne shabdon mein

Aapki dukan mohalle mein sabko pata hai. Par jab koi naya customer phone par "near me" search karta hai, ya koi aapka number dhoondta hai, tab kya dikhta hai? Aaj bahut se customer dukan aane se pehle phone par check karte hain: dukan kab khuli hai, kya milta hai, aur sampark kaise karein.

Is post mein hum simple bhasha mein samjhayenge ki ek dukan ki website mein kya hona chahiye, aur bina coding ke use kaise banwaya ja sakta hai.

## Dukan ko website ki zaroorat kyun hai?

- **Customer ko sahi jaankari milti hai:** timing, address, phone number aur aap kya bechte hain, sab ek jagah.
- **Bharosa banta hai:** asli photos aur purane customers ki baatein dekhkar naya customer aasani se contact karta hai.
- **WhatsApp aur Instagram ke saath kaam aati hai:** bio mein ek link daal do, aur poori jaankari wahan mil jaati hai.
- **Google Business Profile ke saath link:** Google par free profile banane ke baad, website field mein apna page jod sakte hain.

## Ek achhi dukan website mein kya hona chahiye?

Bade-bade pages ki zaroorat nahi. Ek saaf landing page kaafi hai, jisme yeh cheezein hon:

1. **Seedhi headline** – aap kya bechte hain aur kahan. Jaise: "Rajkot mein fasteners aur industrial parts".
2. **Offer ya khaas baat** – aapko doosron se alag kya banata hai.
3. **Products ya services** – mukhya cheezein, chahein to rates ke saath.
4. **Asli photos** – dukan, maal ya kaam ki.
5. **Proof** – customers ki baatein ya jinke saath aapne kaam kiya hai.
6. **Timing aur address** – kab khule hain, kahan hain.
7. **Contact ka aasan tareeka** – phone, WhatsApp ya form ka button.

## Website banane ke teen raaste

**1. Developer se banwana** – achha kaam ho sakta hai, par chhote se change (nayi photo, naya rate, Sunday ki timing) ke liye bhi baar-baar unhe message karna padta hai aur intezaar karna padta hai.

**2. Khud template par banana** – website builder mein khaali template milta hai. Design, text aur settings sab khud samajhna padta hai, jo dukan chalate hue time maangta hai.

**3. Apne shabdon mein batakar banwana (AasaanWeb)** – aap apne business ke baare mein normal bhasha mein batate hain, aur aapke liye poora landing page banta hai.
