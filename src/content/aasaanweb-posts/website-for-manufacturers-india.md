## Why a listing alone often isn't enough
Many small units in Aligarh, Moradabad, Ludhiana, Rajkot or Jamnagar are listed on B2B marketplaces and directories. That's useful. But when a buyer searches your company name, they often land on a listing page that sits next to other sellers' products. Your own page shows only you: your parts, your trade, your phone number.

## What a manufacturer's page should show
1. **What you make, in one line.** For example: "Custom brass parts in Jamnagar."
2. **The real product or machine.** A clear photo of the part, with its features marked, builds more trust than stock images.
3. **Years in the trade and where you are.** Buyers want to know you'll still be there next year.
4. **How to start an order.** Say what's enough to begin: a drawing, a sample, or a small batch.
5. **One-tap contact.** A WhatsApp button for sending drawings, and a call button.
