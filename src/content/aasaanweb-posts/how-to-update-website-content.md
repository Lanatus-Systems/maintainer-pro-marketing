# How to update website content yourself, with a preview before it goes live

Your website is only useful if what it says is true today. New hours, a new phone number, a new photo or a new offer all need to reach the page quickly, and you should be able to make most of these changes yourself.

This guide walks through a simple way to do it, and the mistakes to avoid.

**Quick answer:** To update your website content yourself, list exactly what should change (new hours, a new phone number, a new photo or offer), make the change in your website tool, check it on a preview on your phone, and only then publish. With AasaanWeb you describe the change in plain words, see a preview, and publish it yourself; small changes go live in about 10 minutes.

## Why small updates matter

- Wrong hours or an old phone number cost real enquiries. Customers trust what Google and your site say.
- A site that shows last year's offer looks closed.
- Most updates a shop, clinic or workshop needs are small: hours, phone, a photo, a headline, a button, a FAQ.

## Step by step

1. Write the change down in one sentence ("Change Sunday hours to 10 to 2", "Replace the top photo with the new showroom photo").
2. Collect what's needed: the new photo, the exact number, the exact wording.
3. Make the change. In AasaanWeb, type the request in plain language. You don't need to know how websites are built.
4. Check the preview, ideally on your phone, before anything is public.
5. Publish. Small changes go live in about 10 minutes.
6. Update the same detail on your Google Business Profile and WhatsApp Business profile so all three match.

## What you can change this way

With AasaanWeb you can change the headline, photo, hours, phone, price, button, FAQs and proof sections on your landing page.

## Common mistakes

- Publishing without checking on a phone.
- Changing the site but not the Google Business Profile, so customers see two different hours.
- Leaving expired offers up.
