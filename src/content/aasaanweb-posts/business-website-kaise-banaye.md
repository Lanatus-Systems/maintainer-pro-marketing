# Business website kaise banaye: 4 step guide (bina coding)

**Quick answer:** Business website banane ke 4 step hain: (1) apne business ke baare mein likhiye: kya bechte hain, kiske liye, aur customer aapse kaise sampark kare; (2) bana hua page preview pe dekhiye; (3) jo galat hai woh normal shabdon mein bataiye aur preview pe badalta dekhiye; (4) theek lage tab publish kijiye. AasaanWeb mein yahi 4 step hain, aur baad ka chhota badlav about 10 minutes mein live ho jaata hai.

## Pehle yeh taiyaar rakhiye (10 minute ka kaam)

- Ek line mein: aap kya bechte/banaate hain aur kiske liye.
- 2–4 asli photo: dukan, factory, product, team. Stock photo nahi.
- Timing, address, aur woh number jis pe customer call ya WhatsApp kare.
- Customer ko kya karna chahiye: call, visit, order, ya drawing bhejna.

## Step 1 – Business ke baare mein bataiye

Form ya template ke khaane bharne ki jagah, waise likhiye jaise kisi achhe customer ko batate hain: "Hum Jamnagar mein drawing ke hisaab se brass parts banaate hain, 2001 se." Kya bechte hain, kiske liye hai, aur visitor ko aage kya karna hai, bas itna kaafi hai. Shuru karne ke liye kuch photo kaafi hain.

## Step 2 – Asli page dekhiye

Aapko khaali template nahi, ek poora landing page milta hai: offer, services, proof (photo, customer ki baat), timing aur contact button. Ise customer ki nazar se padhiye, aur ho sake to phone pe.

## Step 3 – Preview pe khud badliye

Jo galat lage, seedha bataiye: "Sunday ki timing 10 se 2 karo", "Upar wali photo nayi dukan wali lagao". Badlav usi preview pe dikhta hai. Headline, photo, timing, phone, price aur button sab aise badal sakte hain. Website kaise banti hai, yeh jaanna zaroori nahi.

## Step 4 – Theek lage tab publish kijiye

Page tab live hota hai jab aap kehte hain. Agle mahine koi chhota badlav ho to wahi preview, wahi tareeka. Naya project shuru karne ki zaroorat nahi.

## Pehle se website hai?

Naya banane ki zaroorat nahi. AasaanWeb mein apni live site connect kijiye, preview kholiye, aur jo galat hai woh likhiye: purani photo, galat number, khatam ho chuka offer. Badlav preview pe dikhega, aur aap publish karenge.

## Aam galtiyaan

- Bina phone pe check kiye publish karna.
- Website pe timing badli, par Google Business Profile pe nahi. Customer ko do alag timing dikhti hain.
- Diwali/sale ka offer khatam hone ke baad bhi page pe chhod dena.
- Sirf IndiaMART ya JustDial page pe nirbhar rehna. Buyer aapka naam Google karta hai to apna page milna chahiye.
