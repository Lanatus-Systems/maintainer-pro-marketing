# Mobile se website kaise banaye – phone par check, plain words mein change

**Quick answer:** Mobile se website banana ka matlab yeh nahi ki aapko phone pe coding karni hai. Pehle business ko plain words mein bataiye — kya bechte ho, kuch photos, timing, aur customer kaise contact kare. Page milne ke baad phone par preview dekhiye (customer jaisa). Jo galat ho, normal shabdon mein likhiye; pehle preview, phir aap publish. Chhote badlaav lagbhag 10 minute mein live ho jaate hain. Yeh post website builder comparison nahi hai — sirf phone-first check aur plain-words update.

## Phone pe zyada kaam kyun hota hai
Dukaan, clinic, studio ya workshop chalane wale din bhar phone pe hote hain: WhatsApp pe drawing aati hai, customer timing poochta hai, naya photo click hota hai. Computer kholna har chhote update ke liye practical nahi. Isliye page aisa hona chahiye jo phone par saaf dikhe, aur badlaav aap short message jaisi language mein de sako — bina coding seekhe.

## Mobile se pehla page: 4 seedhe steps
AasaanWeb par flow aisa hai:

1. **Business batayiye.** Kya bechte ho, kiske liye, customer agla step kya kare (call, visit, WhatsApp, book). Thodi si photos kaafi hain.
2. **Asli page dekhiye.** Offer, services, proof, hours, aur contact wala landing page — blank template nahi.
3. **Preview par theek kariye.** Headline, photo, hours, phone, price, button — jo bhi galat ho, ordinary language mein bataiye jab aap page dekh rahe ho.
4. **Jab theek lage, publish.** Public tabhi hota hai jab aap decide karte ho. Agle hafte ka chhota badlaav bhi usi preview se.

AasaanWeb landing page ke liye bana hai — bade multi-page site ke liye nahi. Shops, studios, clinics aur manufacturers isi audience mein aate hain.

## Phone par preview check
AasaanWeb par owner page ko customer ki tarah phone par padhta hai: change likho, preview phone par check karo, tab publish.

Isliye mobile-first routine yeh hai:
- Preview phone browser mein kholo (jahan customer dekhega).
- Pehli screen: headline, photo, button — thumb se tap karke dekho.
- Hours, phone number, WhatsApp/call button sahi hain ya nahi.
- Galat dikha to change plain words mein do; dubara phone par check; phir publish.

## Badlaav kaise batayein (computer zaroori nahi)
Homepage ke "Say it the way you would say it out loud" examples jaisi lines kaafi hain:

- "Headline same-day repairs ke baare mein banao."
- "Banner ki jagah naya shop photo lagao."
- "Monday band hai — hours se hata do."
- "Booking button phone par pehli screen pe dikhao."
- "Footer ka phone number purana hai. Naya wala use karo."

Jo aap khud badal sakte ho: headline, photo, hours, phone, price, button, aur FAQ/proof sections. Har badlaav pehle preview par aata hai; sirf request se public nahi hota — publish aap karte ho. Chhote badlaav lagbhag 10 minute mein live. Website kaise banti hai yeh jaanna zaroori nahi. Agar Hindi mein madad aasaan ho, to business ya badlaav batate waqt Hindi help available hai.

## Pehle se site hai? Update bhi isi tarah
Agar page pehle se live hai: jo page pehle se live hai use connect karke customer view kholo, jo stale hai woh batao, preview par dekho, phir publish. Menu badla, sale khatam, teen saal purani photo, mobile pe galat number — yeh redesign nahi, chhote corrections hain. Aise chhote badlaav bhi lagbhag 10 minute mein live.

## FAQ
### Mobile se website kaise banaye bina coding?

Business plain words mein batayiye, page preview dekhiye (phone par bhi), jo galat ho woh normal language mein bataiye, phir publish. Coding seekhne ki zaroorat nahi.

### Phone par preview kyun zaroori hai?

Zyada customers pehle phone pe aate hain. Agar button neeche hai, number galat hai, ya photo cut ho rahi hai, woh yahin dikhega — publish se pehle.
